# Licence

## Scores (`scores/`)

Each score was published on MuseScore.com by the person who typed it in, marked either **CC0 1.0** or
**Public Domain**, and was collected in the PDMX dataset (<https://zenodo.org/records/15571083>). The mark of each
score is the `license` field of its entry in `catalog.json`.

- CC0 1.0 Universal: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Public Domain Mark 1.0: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/>

The music itself is by composers who died in 1925 or earlier.

This repository passes those marks on as it found them and cannot guarantee that every one is right. To report a
score that should not be here, open an issue: <https://github.com/mylovejs2/LalaNoteOpenMusic/issues>.
